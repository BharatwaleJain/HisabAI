What happens next?

The app extracts text, tries the configured local Ollama model, and falls back to conservative local patterns if the model is unavailable. You review every field before saving.

Private processing

AI inference runs through your local Ollama service. Your documents are not sent to a remote AI API.

OCR requirement

For image receipts, install Tesseract OCR separately. Text-based PDFs can be read directly; scanned PDFs may need a future OCR pass.
